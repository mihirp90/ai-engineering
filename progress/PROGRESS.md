# Program Progress

Last updated: 2026-10-16

## Status

**Planning:** organized into a 26-week / approximately 390-hour program with a core roadmap and ten section-wise topic learning tracks. **Learning execution:** not started; no labs or flagship application have been implemented yet.

## Program contract

- 26 weeks at about 15 focused hours per week.
- 10 hours/week: core AI curriculum and flagship product.
- 3 hours/week: one selected topic track.
- 1 hour/week: alternating system design/DSA.
- 1 hour/week: progress, learning log and planning.
- Public or appropriately licensed data only; no employer/customer/private data or secrets.
- One main repository is the source of truth.

## Current product decision

Research Intelligence remains a **product hypothesis**, not an approved specification. At Week 8, choose the first user, recurring task, corpus and success metric—or change the product if discovery evidence indicates a better problem. Do not implement a broad cross-domain platform before that decision.
